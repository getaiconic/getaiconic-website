About

I build the system I wish every local business had.

A one-person studio in Chicago, building the booking and follow-up systems local trades actually need.

Chicago · Est. 2026
Annemarie · Founder, AICONIC

I'm Annemarie, 29, originally from Austria and in Chicago for eight years now. AICONIC is a one-person studio. I build and run the booking, follow-up and review systems for trades contractors and small local businesses.

The pattern is always the same. The work is good, the reputation is good, and leads still leak - a call missed while you're with a customer, an enquiry answered a day late, a happy client who never got asked for a review.

None of that is a marketing problem. It's a plumbing problem, and plumbing can be fixed.

So I build one thing properly: a site that books, a phone that always answers, follow-up that doesn't quit, and reviews that arrive on their own. Live in seven to ten days, month to month, and I'm the person you text when you want something changed - not a ticket queue.
